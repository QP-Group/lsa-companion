2. What data we process, and in what role

This product has two distinct kinds of personal data, with two different legal relationships attached. Conflating them would misstate our actual obligations, so they're kept separate here.

2a. Business account data — we are the data controller

Data about the businesses (and their staff) who use LSA Companion directly:

  • Login email address (Supabase Auth)
  • Business name and Google Ads Customer ID
  • Business settings: business hours, alert email, webhook URL if configured
  • The business's own Google Local Services Ads verification/compliance status (e.g. insurance coverage amount and expiry) — this describes the business itself, not their customers

We process this to provide the service under contract with the business.

2b. End-customer lead data — we are a data processor

Data about the business's own customers — the people who contacted a business (e.g. Vertex Property Care) through Google Local Services Ads. This is not our data to decide the purpose of; we process it strictly on the business's instructions, as their processor. The business remains the data controller for their own customers' data.

What's actually stored (confirmed directly against the current database schema, not estimated):

  • Contact phone number
  • Message content / conversation text exchanged with the business
  • Call duration (used only to detect missed calls, not for content)
  • Lead type, status, and timestamps, sourced directly from Google's Local Services Ads platform via the Google Ads API — we never collect this directly from the end customer; it is synced from Google, where the end customer originally submitted it to the business.

We never sell this data, use it for our own marketing, or use it for any purpose beyond operating the service for the business that received the lead.

3. Legal basis for processing

  • Business account data: performance of a contract (providing the service you've signed up for).
  • End-customer lead data: we process this under Article 28-style processor instructions from the business. Our lawful basis derives from the business's own lawful basis with their customer (typically legitimate interest or contract — e.g. responding to a quote enquiry), not a separate basis of our own.

4. Who else processes this data (sub-processors)

  • Supabase (database, authentication, backend functions) — hosted in the EU (eu-west-1, Ireland), confirmed directly against the live project configuration.
  • Google LLC — the original source of all end-customer lead data, via the Google Ads API. Google's own privacy terms govern their side of this.
  • Resend — used to send email alerts (new-lead notifications, spend/compliance alerts) to the business. These emails can contain lead contact details. TO CONFIRM: Resend's data residency and whether an adequate international-transfer mechanism (e.g. SCCs) is in place has not been independently verified — check Resend's own DPA/subprocessor terms before treating this as settled.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts only the static dashboard frontend (HTML/JS/CSS). It never receives or stores lead data itself; the dashboard talks directly to Supabase from the browser.

5. Where data is hosted

Primary database and backend: Supabase, eu-west-1 (Ireland) — confirmed directly, not assumed.

6. Data retention

Policy commitment: end-customer lead data is retained for 90 days after a business disconnects from LSA Companion (businesses.status = 'disconnected'), then permanently deleted.

Enforced as of 2026-08-20. A daily job (purge_disconnected_business_data(), scheduled via Supabase Cron at 03:00 UTC) deletes leads rows — and their associated messages, via cascade — for any business disconnected more than 90 days. The business's own account row is not deleted, only its former customers' lead data. Verified with synthetic test data (never real customer data): a business disconnected 100 days ago had its lead correctly purged (cascade-deleting the message too), a business disconnected only 10 days ago was correctly left untouched, and an audit record (data_retention_purges) was correctly created for the purge and correctly absent for the non-purge. Real Vertex data confirmed untouched throughout.

7. Security measures

  • Row-level security (RLS) on every table holding business or lead data, scoped to business_members — a user can only ever see data for businesses they belong to.
  • Google account refresh tokens are never stored in plain text — encrypted at rest via Supabase Vault, accessed only through a locked-down security definer function.
  • All traffic over HTTPS.
  • No personal data is placed in URL query strings or logs by design.

8. Data subject rights

If you are an end customer who contacted a business via Google Local Services Ads, and that business uses LSA Companion: the business you contacted is the correct first point of contact for any request to access, correct, or delete your data, since they are the data controller. If you contact us directly, we will support that request and relay/action it in coordination with the relevant business, as required of a processor.

If you are a business using LSA Companion directly: contact enpee.sides@gmail.com for any request regarding your own account data.

9. International transfers

Primary hosting (Supabase) is within the EU. Google (source data) and Resend (email delivery) are separate processors with their own international-transfer position — see section 4's TO CONFIRM note on Resend specifically before relying on this section as complete.
