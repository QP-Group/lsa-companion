LSA Companion

A lead inbox for businesses running Google Local Services Ads.

What it does

LSA Companion connects to a business's Google Local Services Ads account and pulls incoming leads — calls and messages — into a single inbox. It automatically replies to new message leads with a booking link, alerts the business by email when a new lead comes in, and flags things that need a human's attention: missed calls, spend anomalies, and upcoming compliance deadlines like insurance verification expiry.

Who it's for

Businesses that advertise through Google Local Services Ads and want their leads in one place, with faster first-response times than checking the Google Ads app manually.

What it accesses

With a business's permission, LSA Companion reads that business's own Local Services Ads lead and conversation data from the Google Ads API, and can send a reply on the business's behalf to a lead that messaged them. It never accesses any other part of a Google Ads account, and never contacts a lead unless the business's own automated reply settings are turned on.
